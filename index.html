CARE RITUALS

Preserving Your 925 Silver

A few simple rituals ensure your silver radiates with pristine brilliance forever.

01

Wear Often

Your skin’s natural oils actually help keep 925 sterling silver clean and glowing. Regular wear is its best polish.

02

Keep It Dry

While our pieces are water-resistant, we recommend patting them dry after showers and keeping them away from chlorinated pools.

03

Store in Velvet

Always return your bracelets to their provided airtight moisture-wicking pouch and Mahesh Jewellers velvet case.

04

Gentle Polishing

Use our complimentary micro-suede polishing cloth with light circular strokes to restore that showroom platinum luster.

PRECISION FIT ATELIER

Wrist Sizing & Measurement Guide

Follow our simple 2-step ritual to determine your perfect bracelet or kada dimension.

Step 1

Measure Your Wrist

Wrap a flexible measuring tape or strip of paper snugly around your wrist bone. Mark where it meets.

Step 2

Add Comfort Allowance

For a snug fit, add 0.5 cm (0.2"). For a relaxed drape, add 1.5 cm (0.6"). Match to the chart below.

Wrist Circumference Recommended Size Collection Fit
14.0 - 15.5 cm (5.5" - 6.1") Small (56 mm) Women's Dainty / Petite
15.6 - 17.0 cm (6.2" - 6.7") Standard (58 mm / 7.0") Universal Women's / Slim Men's
17.1 - 18.5 cm (6.8" - 7.3") Medium (62 mm / 7.5") Men's Standard / Women's Relaxed
18.6 - 20.5 cm (7.4" - 8.1") Large (65 mm / 8.0") Men's Heavy Cuban & Kada
